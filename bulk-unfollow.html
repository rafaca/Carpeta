Leaving Jonestown

Bulk unfollow / unfriend for Instagram, Facebook & Threads.

Browser-console scripts for cleaning out your social graph. Runs entirely in your own logged-in browser tab. No server, no credentials handled here.

Read before using.
  • Bulk automation violates Meta's Terms of Service. Your account can be temporarily action-blocked or permanently disabled.
  • There is no public API for these actions. The scripts call the same internal endpoints / DOM elements your browser uses when you click buttons manually.
  • Use a conservative pace. The defaults run a few hundred actions per session with 3–8 s random delays. Increase only if you understand the risk.
  • Facebook changes its DOM frequently — those scripts may need selector tweaks if they stop finding buttons.
  • Operate only on accounts you own.

Pace settings

These values are interpolated into each snippet below. After running, type window.STOP_BULK = true in the console to abort early.

Instagram — unfollow everyone you follow

  1. Open instagram.com in this browser and make sure you're logged in.
  2. Open DevTools (F12 or Cmd-Option-I) → Console tab.
  3. Click Copy, paste in the console, press Enter.
  4. Watch progress. To stop: window.STOP_BULK = true.

Instagram — remove all your followers

  1. On instagram.com, logged in.
  2. Open DevTools → Console.
  3. Paste the snippet, press Enter.
  4. Removed followers will need to re-request to follow you (private accounts) or re-tap follow (public).

Facebook — unfriend everyone

  1. Open facebook.com/friends/list and let it load.
  2. Open DevTools → Console.
  3. Paste the snippet, press Enter. The script scrolls to load more friends, then clicks each "Friends" menu → "Unfriend" → confirm.
  4. If FB has changed its markup and nothing happens, the selectors at the top of the script need updating.

Facebook — unfollow everyone (people & pages)

  1. Open facebook.com/friends/following.
  2. Open DevTools → Console.
  3. Paste the snippet, press Enter. Unfollowing keeps people as friends but removes them from your feed.

Threads — unfollow everyone

  1. Open threads.com and log in. Navigate to your profile, then open the Following list (the count near your bio).
  2. Open DevTools → Console.
  3. Paste the snippet, press Enter. The script scrolls the list and clicks each "Following" pill, then confirms.

Threads — remove all your followers

  1. On your Threads profile, open the Followers list.
  2. Open DevTools → Console.
  3. Paste the snippet. It opens each follower's row menu and clicks "Remove".
