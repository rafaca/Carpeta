# Curbitas outreach templates

Copy-ready message templates, one per channel type. Every one is meant to be
**personalized before you send it** — swap the `{{placeholders}}`, cut anything
that doesn't fit, and make it sound like you. Generic copy-paste is what gets
flagged as spam; a specific, human note is what gets a yes.

Placeholders:
- `{{name}}` — the target's name
- `{{neighborhood}}` — their neighborhood, if relevant
- `{{your_name}}` — you
- `{{hook}}` — the one specific reason curbitas fits *this* audience

---

## newsletter_pitch
> For newsletters, blogs, and tip lines (The Skint, Brokelyn, Gothamist).
> These channels *invite* submissions — this is the front door, use it.

**Subject:** A free tool for finding curb treasures in {{neighborhood}}

Hi {{name}} team,

I read {{name}} for {{hook}}, so I thought this might fit. I built
**curbitas.com** — a free tool that helps New Yorkers find and share
good stuff left on the curb before it hits the landfill. It's basically
stooping culture, organized.

No ask beyond: if it seems useful to your readers, I'd love for you to
take a look. Happy to write a short blurb or answer anything.

Thanks for everything you put out,
{{your_name}} · curbitas.com

## reddit_comment
> For subreddits. NEVER a standalone ad. Only use inside a thread where
> someone is genuinely asking about free/secondhand stuff, as a helpful reply.
> Disclose that you made it. Read the sub's self-promo rule first.

For furniture, honestly the best free sources are stooping season, Buy
Nothing, and curb alerts. Full disclosure, I built a little tool for
exactly this — curbitas.com — that maps curb finds around the city. Not
trying to spam, just genuinely what I use for this. Good luck with the
{{hook}}!

## neighbor_intro
> For Nextdoor, from a real verified neighbor account, in the free-items /
> recommendations context. One neighborhood at a time.

Hi neighbors — sharing something I've been using for {{neighborhood}}:
curbitas.com, a free way to spot and share curb finds nearby so good
furniture gets a second home instead of the trash. Thought some of you
who love a good stoop find might enjoy it.

## partnership_pitch
> For accounts/orgs with big overlapping audiences (Stooping NYC, community
> orgs). You're proposing mutual value, not asking for a favor.

Hi {{name}},

Huge fan of what you do — {{hook}}. I run curbitas.com, a free tool for
finding and sharing curb finds around NYC. Our audiences are basically
the same people who love giving good stuff a second life.

Would you be open to a collab? A few ideas: a cross-post, featuring the
best finds each week, or something you'd rather do. Whatever's genuinely
useful to your community. No budget ask — just think we could do more
together.

{{your_name}} · curbitas.com

## none
> For Buy Nothing groups and any `no_promo` target: there is no template,
> because you do not post promo here. Join, gift, receive, be helpful. If an
> admin or member ever asks what you use to find curb stuff, you can mention
> curbitas honestly — but you never lead with it. Goodwill is the strategy.
