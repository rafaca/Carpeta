01 / AI Practice

Friction as Feature

Zoë Scaman built a guided product for encoding your own creative practice into 8 to 10 AI skill files, so the work stays distinctively yours rather than converging on the model's defaults.

Built in Lovable, launching in three to four weeks at premium pricing. The process runs through surfacing, interrogation, naming, and encoding. Scaman argues the skill files don't replace the work; they put you to work: sparring with your own mind, mining your own memory. What you walk out with is unmistakably yours. link

02 / Culture

Fungible Art

Haley Nahman found herself rating a film the moment the credits rolled, like rating a mop on Amazon, and traced the trained reflex back to Letterboxd and Goodreads.

"Neither good nor bad, things that are mid are fungible."Isabel Pabán Freed, Everything's Fine

Isabel Pabán Freed's essay on "mid" art supplies the structural argument: star ratings flatten works into commodities better suited to the market, selecting for art that translates cleanly into a score. Replace a mid novel with any other mid novel, Freed writes, and the experience holds. Nahman argues that conclusiveness becomes the reward, the appraisal becomes an identity project, and the work's lifespan shrinks to the verdict. link

03 / Tools

Instrument of Command

Studio Self runs a solo 6-figure agency on a single Moleskine notebook, not a Notion command centre or a 47-column Airtable base. The notebook is the instrument of command; everything else is downstream of it.

The argument: a solo agency concentrates the entire company into one nervous system, and software keeps everything while a notebook keeps only what matters. Feed everything into software and you preserve every request. A notebook forces the daily decision about what earns attention. Studio Self writes outcomes, never tasks: an outcome is "client has a clear answer"; a task is just "reply to client." link

04 / Brand

Visibility Without Consideration

Zara dressed Bad Bunny at the Met Gala with BENITO ANTONIO, a 150-piece collection developed with his longtime creative director Janthony Oliveras, previewed at the Super Bowl Halftime Show and a surprise store takeover in San Juan.

But brand consideration fell 6 percentage points over the past year, from 21% to 15%, and preference dropped from 7% to 5%. Tracksuit data, cited by Brand Waves, points to a structural challenge in fast fashion rather than a campaign failure: high-visibility cultural moments are not translating into consideration or preference gains. link

05 / AI Work

Verification Is Labor

WEF projects 170 million jobs created against 92 million displaced between 2025 and 2030, a net gain of 78 million. The least discussed job category in that forecast: verification.

As AI generates more reports, code, forecasts, and legal drafts, PM Researcher argues someone must evaluate whether those outputs are accurate, lawful, and useful. Microsoft research finds workers already rank quality control of AI output as an increasingly important skill. The net-positive forecast depends on verification work expanding fast enough to absorb what automation displaces. link
