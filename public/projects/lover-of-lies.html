The Lover of Lies

An original immersive fiction staged across three rooms and one telephone line. Audiences arrive as guests of a memorial service for a man who may not be dead — and leave having decided, together, what the truth gets to be.

Built on our conviction that play is the oldest form of human intelligence, The Lover of Lies turns spectators into accomplices: every scene is a game with hidden rules, and every lie told inside it is a door.

Every lie told inside it is a door — the audience decides what the truth gets to be.

Role: concept, narrative design, spatial design, game systems, production. Format: 70 minutes, 24 guests per run. First staged 2026, New York.

The piece ran for six weeks and sold out its final fortnight by word of mouth alone — the ending has never been printed, and the room keeps its secrets.
